## 1. The Challenge: Running a Growing Business on Paper

Today, the business runs on manual records: notebooks, handwritten vouchers, loose receipts, and spreadsheets passed around between staff. It works — until it doesn't.

**The hidden costs of manual bookkeeping:**

- **Hours lost every week** re-writing the same figures into ledgers, summaries and reports.
- **Human error** — a mistyped amount, a missed entry, a wrong total — that is only discovered weeks later, if ever.
- **No real-time view.** The owner only knows the true cash position after someone compiles the numbers by hand.
- **Branches are blind spots.** Each location keeps its own records; comparing them is slow and unreliable.
- **Missed due dates.** Checks to suppliers and collections from customers slip through because no one is reminded.
- **Weak controls.** Anyone can change a paper record. There is no trail of who changed what, and when.
- **Lost or damaged records.** Paper burns, gets wet, gets misplaced. A spreadsheet on one laptop disappears with that laptop.
- **Slow approvals.** Vouchers wait on someone's desk for a signature.

**TARTAR BMS was built to remove every one of these problems.**

## 4. Core Features

### 4.2 Transactions — One Record, Many Uses

Every movement of money is captured in one place: sales, customer payments, supplier payments, cash deposits, petty cash and collections.

- Tracks **where the money went**: cash drawer or bank account.
- Tracks **income source**: product sales, rental income and more.

### 4.3 Sales with Deposit Verification

Sales follow a controlled, three-step process that protects the business:

1. **Employee records the sale.**
2. **Employee marks it Deposited**, with the deposit date.
3. **Admin verifies** after checking that the bank was actually credited — or **rejects** it and sends it back to the employee for correction.

- Sales entered by an admin are verified immediately.
- Unverified sales are clearly marked **"Pending verification"** and kept out of the official sales totals until confirmed.

> **Benefit:** the owner knows every reported sale has actually reached the bank. Discrepancies surface immediately, not at audit time.

---

### 4.4 Purchases and Expenses — With Automatic Vouchers

Purchases and operating expenses each have their own dedicated module. **Every purchase and every expense automatically generates its voucher** — it is impossible to record a cost without one.

- Expenses are organised by **category** (salaries, electricity, water, internet, office supplies, repairs & maintenance, taxes, and any category the business adds).
- Purchases can carry a **due date** for credit terms (e.g. 60-day checks); once approved, they automatically appear in **Payables** and in the dashboard alerts.
- Payee can be a registered supplier or a one-time payee.

---

### 4.5 Vouchers — Numbered, Computed, Approved, Printed

The voucher system replaces handwritten check and cash vouchers entirely.

**Automatic numbering**
Each voucher receives a unique number that identifies the branch, the category, and the year — for example **HAR-PUR-2026-00000187**. Numbers are assigned by the system, in sequence, per branch and category. **No duplicates, no gaps, no manual tracking.**

**Automatic computation**
The user enters the invoice amount, the return (if any), and the withholding rate (None, 1% or 2%). The system computes:

- the amount before VAT,
- the withholding tax,
- the **final amount to pay**.

The computed withholding can still be adjusted by hand for special cases.

**Check vouchers vs. cash vouchers**
The voucher type follows the payment source: bank account → check voucher, cash drawer → cash voucher. Check vouchers require the **bank, check number and check date** — they cannot be saved incomplete.

**Approval workflow**
Vouchers move through **Pending → Approved / Rejected**. Only managers approve. Once a voucher is approved or printed, its source transaction is **locked** and can no longer be edited or deleted — enforced by the database itself, not just the screen.

**Printable vouchers**
Professionally formatted, ready-to-sign vouchers printed with the branch's legal name and address as the letterhead.

> **Benefit:** faster approvals, correct tax computation every time, and vouchers that can't be altered after the fact.

## 7. Security and Control

- **Individual logins** for every user — no shared passwords.
- **Role-based permissions** enforced both on screen and inside the database itself.
- **Row-level security:** the database only returns the records each user is allowed to see.
